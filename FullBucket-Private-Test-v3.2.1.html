Why Full Bucket

I knew why I was doing all of this.

For most of my life, responsibility made sense: family, work, people depending on me, things that needed to get done. The question I was much slower to ask was: What about me?

During an intensive behavioral-health program, one useful exercise was values clarification. But identifying the words wasn't the hardest part. The harder questions came afterward: What actually supports those values? What puts something back in my bucket? And in a life that's already full, where does any of it fit?

Full Bucket can connect the dots. You make the choice.
