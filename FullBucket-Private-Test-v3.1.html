Why Full Bucket

I knew why I was doing all of this.

For most of my life, responsibility made sense to me: family, work, people depending on me, things that needed to get done. The question I was much slower to ask was: What about me?

During an intensive behavioral-health program, one of the useful exercises I encountered was values clarification. I narrowed a large stack of values down to the few that mattered most to me. But identifying the words wasn't the hardest part.

The harder questions came afterward: What actually supports those values in my life? What puts something back in my bucket? And in a day that's already full, am I making room for any of it?

Full Bucket doesn't tell you how to live. It helps you connect those dots. Full Bucket can connect the dots. You make the choice.
